# 1. Purpose

This document defines the structure, navigation, and organization of the portfolio website.

The goal is to ensure visitors can quickly understand who Ashuraj is, what he specializes in, explore his work, and easily get in touch.

The navigation should be intuitive, minimal, and require as few clicks as possible.
